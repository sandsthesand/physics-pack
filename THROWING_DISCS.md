# Throwing Disc resource assets

`pack1.zip` includes the client assets from **Java Throwing Discs Backport 1.4**, by **gluet2**, supplied as `throwing_discs_backport-1.4.jar`.

Project: https://modrinth.com/datapack/throwing_discs_backport
Original declared license: `LicenseRef-All-Rights-Reserved`. Original authorship and rights remain with the author.

Only its `assets/throwing_disc/` files were added: 6 item definitions, 10 item models and 10 textures. Existing pack entries and 26.3 resource metadata were preserved byte-for-byte. The mod wrapper's datapack-format metadata was not used as resource-pack metadata.

This ZIP supplies visuals, not gameplay. The throwing-disc datapack/mod must be installed separately on the server. Vanilla players need no client mod; they need to accept the resource pack.
